Active case warning

Korean player / Canada Junior A camp

Do not send a Korean player to a sanctioned camp, tryout, practice, exhibition, or regular-season activity until the required federation and league permission is confirmed in writing. A recent case showed that a player may be held off the ice when a league commissioner needs a Korean Hockey Federation permission letter for a 15-day tryout.

Operating rule

Use primary sources only for rules conclusions. If a source is incomplete, contradictory, or not specific to Korea, mark the answer as needing a phone call or written governing-body confirmation.
